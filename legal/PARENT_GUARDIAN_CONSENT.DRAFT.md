# Parent / Guardian Consent Working Draft

This template is an input for legal review, not a consent form ready for use. It
does not claim legal validity or compliance with any law. No option may be
pre-selected, and refusing an optional purpose must not be treated as agreement.

## Proposed plain-language explanation

The club would like to use short swimming video clips in Swim Sight 3D to help a
coach review technique and prepare feedback. The service may process the video
into pose keypoints, processing telemetry, AI-assisted draft findings, coach
notes, cues, drills, and a swimmer report.

AI-assisted findings are drafts. They are reviewed, edited, approved, or
rejected by a coach. They are not automatic coaching decisions.

## People and consent evidence

The lawyer-approved form should capture:

- the identified child or swimmer the permission covers;
- parent or guardian full name;
- relationship to the swimmer and confirmation of authority to consent;
- contact details;
- the club and coach or staff member collecting consent;
- form version and the exact notices presented;
- signature or affirmative electronic action, with date and time; and
- how consent was recorded, changed, or withdrawn.

The production database currently stores a limited internal consent record.
The lawyer must decide which evidence belongs in the product, the club's own
system, or a separate controlled record. Guardian contact must never appear in
a public shared report.

## Separate choices — none pre-selected

The final form should present these as distinct choices rather than one bundled
permission. Legal review must decide which are necessary for the requested
service and which are optional.

- `[ ]` Permission to record the specified swimming footage.
- `[ ]` Permission to upload and store that footage for private club review.
- `[ ]` Permission to use optional AI-assisted processing to produce draft
  evidence for coach review.
- `[ ]` Permission for the coach to create a report using approved content.
- `[ ]` Permission to share that report with the specifically named swimmer,
  parent, guardian, coach, or club recipient by a controlled report link.

The form must explain the practical effect of declining each choice. A coach
must not infer permission for one purpose from permission for another.

## Training, research, and marketing are separate purposes

The choices above do not permit footage, pose keypoints, findings, or reports to
be used to train or fine-tune an AI model, build a research dataset, advertise
the product, or create marketing material. Any future proposal for those uses
requires a separate notice, fresh legal review, and separate specific opt-in.
It must not be bundled with access to ordinary coach review.

## Retention in plain language

The current technical default selects raw footage for deletion after the
coach-review workflow is complete or after 30 days, whichever occurs first.
The final form must state the lawyer-approved period, explain that approved
reports may have a different period, and explain provider backup and log
retention honestly.

## Withdrawal and existing reports

The final form must give a simple withdrawal method using
`[PRIVACY/WITHDRAWAL CONTACT]`. It must explain:

- when new recording, upload, AI processing, and sharing will stop;
- whether an existing shared link will be revoked;
- whether an existing coach-approved report will be deleted or retained;
- what limited audit evidence may remain;
- how provider backups and logs age out; and
- that withdrawal does not automatically resolve every past lawful use.

The current technical gate can block new AI processing for a minor whose
consent is missing or withdrawn. It does not replace the club's human process.
