# Parent / Guardian Consent Working Draft

This template is an input for legal review, not a consent form ready for use. It
does not claim legal validity or compliance with any particular law.

## Proposed plain-language explanation

The club would like to use short swimming video clips in Swim Sight 3D to help a
coach review technique and prepare feedback. The service may process the video
into pose keypoints, processing telemetry, AI-assisted draft findings, coach
notes, cues, drills, and a swimmer report.

AI-assisted findings are drafts. They are reviewed, edited, approved, or
rejected by a coach. They are not automatic coaching decisions.

## Information the consent record may contain

- Whether the swimmer is a minor.
- Consent status: none, granted, or withdrawn.
- Parent or guardian name and email.
- The date consent was granted or withdrawn.
- The source of consent, such as a lawyer-approved club form or process.

Guardian contact is an internal safeguarding record and must not appear in a
public shared report.

## Proposed choices for legal review

- Permission to record or upload the agreed swimming footage.
- Permission to use it for private coach review and AI-assisted processing.
- Permission to create and share a coach-approved report with the intended
  swimmer, parent, guardian, or authorised club recipient.
- A process for asking questions or withdrawing consent.

## Retention and withdrawal

The current technical default removes raw footage after the coach-review
workflow is complete or after 30 days, whichever occurs first. An authorised
erasure request removes scoped private footage and AI artifacts while retaining
a limited audit marker. Withdrawing consent blocks new AI processing when the
production consent gate is enabled; the lawyer-approved process must define how
existing records and reports are handled.

## Details the lawyer must supply

The final form requires the responsible organisation's identity, contact
details, purpose wording, signature or electronic-consent method, withdrawal
process, retention terms, jurisdiction-specific notices, and any required
records for minors.
