# Private Coach Pilot Runbook

## Pilot objective

Validate whether coaches understand and trust the workflow enough to use Swim Sight 3D again.

This is an internal workflow pilot. It does not prove AI performance and must not be presented as proof of analysis quality. AI-assisted findings remain drafts until a coach approves, edits, or rejects them.

## Before the session

- Confirm consent is recorded before processing footage. For a minor, confirm guardian consent.
- Use one or two swimmers and aliases in pilot notes.
- Prepare one clean, well-lit 5–15 second clip with one swimmer clearly visible.
- Confirm the private upload and Coach Studio workflow opens on the test device.
- Keep a manual coach review available if the evidence level is limited.
- Do not share any report until it is finalised and coach-approved.

## 15-minute pilot script

### 0–2 minutes: explain the workflow

Explain that Swim Sight 3D provides AI-assisted draft findings. The coach remains responsible for reviewing the video and deciding whether each suggestion should be approved, edited, or rejected.

### 2–5 minutes: show upload and review context

Open the prepared clip, show its status, and explain that short clean footage is preferred. Show where the coach can continue manual review when AI evidence is limited or needs coach review.

### 5–10 minutes: coach reviews AI drafts

Ask the coach to inspect the source video, open “Why this finding?”, and decide on every draft. Observe whether the evidence frame, confidence label, editing controls, and reject action are understandable without prompting.

### 10–13 minutes: show the final report

Show only coach-approved findings, selected key moments, Coach Draw annotations, cues, drills, and next focus. Run the pilot report safety summary and open a generated shared link while logged out.

### 13–15 minutes: ask feedback questions

Complete the local pilot notes and structured feedback form. Ask what the coach trusted, rejected, found confusing, and what would need to change before they would pay or use the product with a squad.

## Success signals

- Coach approves or edits findings without confusion.
- Coach understands AI content is draft-only.
- Coach says the report would help parents or swimmers.
- Coach wants to test another swimmer.
- Coach asks about using it at squad or club level.

## Failure signals

- Coach does not trust the findings.
- Coach cannot tell what evidence the AI used.
- Report feels too generic.
- Sharing or report flow feels unsafe or confusing.
- Coach would not use the product again.

## Strict privacy rules

- Use swimmer aliases in pilot notes and feedback.
- Do not record guardian details in feedback.
- Do not include medical information.
- Do not expose raw videos publicly.
- Do not share reports until coach-approved.
- For minors, confirm guardian consent before any footage is processed.
- Do not paste private video links, internal payloads, or technical debug output into pilot notes.

## After the session

1. Save the local session notes and feedback.
2. Revoke any share link that is no longer needed.
3. Record workflow bugs separately from opinions about draft finding quality.
4. Decide whether the signal is strong enough to test another swimmer.
5. Treat all feedback as product validation only; it does not train the AI automatically.
