# How To Film A Swim Review

Use short, clear footage so the coach can review the swimmer efficiently.

## Recommended capture

- Film from the side, above the water.
- Use good, even light and avoid strong backlighting.
- Keep one swimmer in frame.
- Record 5–15 seconds for the first review.
- Keep the swimmer's full body visible where possible.
- Hold the camera steady and avoid digital zoom during the clip.
- Prefer a normal MP4 or MOV recording at 720p or 1080p.

## Before recording or uploading

- Follow the club's lawyer-approved safeguarding and consent process.
- For a swimmer marked as a minor, confirm guardian consent has been recorded.
- Explain the agreed purpose and who will receive the coach-approved report.
- Do not include unrelated swimmers where practical.
- Do not put real names in video filenames.

## What to expect

Swim Sight 3D provides a coach-review workflow with optional AI-assisted draft
findings. Video quality can affect pose evidence. The coach reviews the footage,
decides what is supported, and approves the final report content.
